The criteria were not relaxed after the results were seen. The selected checkpoint is preserved and recommended for the owner's decision (section 9).

## 8. What these numbers do and do not show

* On `modern_test` the E3 models detect almost all SFHQ and DeepDetect fakes while Model 4 and v2 detect almost none; Celeb-DF video accuracy is unchanged (0.98); DeepFakeFace detection is about as poor as Model 4's, and clearly worse than v2's.
* `modern_test` is **not** a generator-disjoint test: its fakes come from the same generator families as the training fakes (DeepDetect has no per-image generator labels), and its reals are FFHQ only, so a real-source/fake-source style shortcut cannot be excluded. The only unseen-generator test is DeepFakeFace `test_crossgen`, where E3 does **not** improve on Model 4.
* Therefore the high `modern_test` accuracy must **not** be read as a general ability to detect modern AI images or video. No labelled modern AI-video data was available, so nothing is claimed about AI video.
* Frame-level results are on face crops from three real-image sources and a handful of generators; the intervals above reflect sampling error only, not dataset bias.
